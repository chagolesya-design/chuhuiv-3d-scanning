03

CONCEPT

DELAYED
RE-CREATION

A new form of cultural heritage preservation for situations where physical restoration is currently impossible.

  1. DELAYED RE-CREATION

    Develop and substantiate the concept as a new strategy for the sustainable preservation of cultural heritage under conditions of radical loss.

  2. DIGITAL AUTHENTICITY

    Define its role in ensuring the continuity of cultural memory.

  3. DIGITAL DOCUMENTATION

    Carry out 3D scanning and build a digital model of a selected architectural heritage site in Chuhuiv.

    3D SCANNING · PHOTOGRAMMETRY · GIS

  4. METHODOLOGY

    Elaborate methodological principles of digital re-creation that integrate technical data with cultural logic and authenticity.

  5. VALIDATION AND OUTREACH

    Test the concept in the public domain (seminar, academic publications, the “Chuhuiv Declaration of Delayed Re-creation”) and establish it within academic and museum communities.
